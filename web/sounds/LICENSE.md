# Sound licence

Every file in this folder is derived from Kenney's CC0 packs on OpenGameArt: [Casino Audio](https://opengameart.org/content/54-casino-sound-effects-cards-dice-chips) (move, capture, promote) and [Interface Sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/interface-sounds) (check, start, end, low time). CC0 needs no attribution. We credit Kenney (www.kenney.nl) anyway.

The originals were trimmed, peak-matched per group and converted to mono MP3 with ffmpeg.
